The longest and most open piece of work in the course. Choose a case —
one from the syllabus, one from your case dossier, or the live case from
week 11 — and argue a specific position about it using the framework built
across the semester: what was actually lost, what kind of loss it was, and
what, if anything, would have caught it earlier.

Week 11 deliberately supplies no verdict of its own. If you write on it,
the verdict is yours to reach and yours to defend — not to borrow from the
week page.
