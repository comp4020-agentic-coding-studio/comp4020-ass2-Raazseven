## the claim

Lisanne Bainbridge's 1983 paper: automate a task well enough, and the
person nominally supervising it stops practising the underlying skill,
because the automation rarely needs them to use it. Reliability is exactly
what erodes the skill that failure will call on.

```notes
Nobody has to choose this the way week 6's deskilling was chosen — that's
the contrast worth drawing before the diagram.
```
