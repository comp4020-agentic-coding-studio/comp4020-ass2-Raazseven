## the claim

An organisation can hold every drawing, every specification, every report it
ever produced about how it once did something, and still be unable to do
that thing. "We know how we did it" can mean "we have the file," or it can
mean "we could do it again tomorrow" — most institutions cannot tell you
which one they mean until the capability is needed and turns out not to be
there.

## the question this week leaves for the seminar

If a capability and its record can come apart, what would it take to notice
the gap *before* you need the capability and find it isn't there?
