## the claim

NASA held the original F-1 drawings decades after production ended and
still could not straightforwardly rebuild the engine from them. Engineers
went back to physical hardware — recovered flight-hardware injector plates,
museum-held engines — and reverse-engineered tolerances, weld sequences, and
manufacturing judgement calls the drawings alone never settled.
