## the claim

Harry Braverman argued the twentieth-century separation of "conception"
from "execution" was a management strategy, not a side effect of machinery.
Judgement moved upward and skill was narrowed downward on purpose, because a
narrower job is cheaper to staff and easier to supervise.

```notes
"The capability was lost" and "the capability was given away" can describe
the same event from two different chairs in the same building — worth
saying before the diagram, not after.
```

## the question this week leaves for the seminar

If a capability is deliberately narrowed for good reasons at the time, at
what point does "narrowed on purpose" become indistinguishable, later, from
"lost by neglect"?
