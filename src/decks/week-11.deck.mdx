## generative models and knowledge work

---

## the case for a loss in progress

Generative models can now produce competent first drafts of writing, code,
and analysis that a person used to have to produce themselves in order to
build the underlying judgement. Weeks 6 and 7 both show a capability can
erode without anyone deciding to erode it — if that pattern holds here, the
tacit knowledge built by doing the work yourself may not survive when the
output is increasingly all that gets produced.

---

## the case against

Every automation this course has studied changed what skill was needed
without necessarily reducing the total skill in the system. Editing,
prompting, and judging model output at scale may be a real capability being
built right now, not a gap opening up.
