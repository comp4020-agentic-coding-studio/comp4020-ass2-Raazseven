## the claim

In the early 1960s, several physics labs tried to build a TEA laser from the
published literature alone. The papers were not wrong or withheld. Harry
Collins found that success tracked one thing: contact with a lab that
already had one working. The papers travelled freely. The ability to build
the thing they described did not.

## the question this week leaves for the seminar

If some knowledge is tacit by its nature — never fully write-downable — is
"we should have documented it better" ever a complete answer to a lost
capability?
