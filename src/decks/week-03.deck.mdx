# Records without capability

## complete records, absent capability

{/* _class: impact */}

---

## the claim

A procedure manual can pass every audit — every step present, every
sign-off recorded — and still fail the moment someone who has never done the
thing tries to follow it cold. Completeness is a property of documents, not
of practices, and "we have full records" is a claim about the archive, not
about what the organisation can currently do.

```notes
The two claims get confused constantly, usually by whoever is relying on
the records rather than whoever wrote them. Worth naming that pair out
loud before the diagram.
```

## the question this week leaves for the seminar

If a record can be complete and still insufficient, what would a record
that closed the gap even look like — and is "more detail" the right axis to
add it on?
