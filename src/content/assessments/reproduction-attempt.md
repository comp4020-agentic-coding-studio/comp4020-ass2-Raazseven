Pick something ordinary that's documented somewhere — a recipe, a craft
technique, an assembly instruction, a piece of folk repair knowledge — and
try to do it using only the documentation, with no help from anyone who
already knows how. Then write up exactly where the documentation held and
where it didn't.

This is the F-1 and FOGBANK cases run at a scale you can actually attempt
yourself, on something with no stakes attached. The report matters more
than the outcome: a failed attempt that names its exact gap precisely is a
better piece of work than a success that can't explain why it worked.
