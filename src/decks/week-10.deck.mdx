# Detection

## diagnosis after the fact, or detection in progress

{/* _class: impact */}

---

## the claim

Every case so far in this course was noticed after the fact — the
capability was needed and turned out not to be there. This week turns the
method around: what would evidence of a loss in progress look like while it
was still forming, rather than after it had already cost years and a
rebuilding budget?

```notes
Be honest that this is harder than diagnosis: the record track and
capability track are read from documents that describe a state, not from a
live signal. Say that before the diagram.
```
