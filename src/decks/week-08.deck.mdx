## the claim

In 2015 the US Naval Academy reinstated instruction in celestial
navigation, dropped decades earlier once satellite positioning made it seem
unnecessary. Public reporting is unusually explicit about both why it came
back and what it cost — new instruction hours, a specific class year, and
instructors who had to relearn a skill the institution had spent decades
treating as obsolete.

```notes
Most of this course documents a loss. This is the one case with numbers
attached to buying the capability back — worth pausing on before the
diagram.
```
