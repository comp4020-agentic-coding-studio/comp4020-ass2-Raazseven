## a loss claim is a claim, not a fact

---

## the claim

"The secret of Damascus steel was lost" and "Stradivari's varnish can never
be reproduced" are two of the most repeated claims in this genre — and both
are contested by historians and materials scientists. A loss claim needs
the same evidentiary standard as any other historical assertion; a
compelling story is not evidence.
